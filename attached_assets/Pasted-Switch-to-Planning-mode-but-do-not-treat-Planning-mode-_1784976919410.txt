## Workstream 6 — Unify Tasks and Actions

Keep both pages, but give them distinct purposes.

Tasks:
Operational work being performed by a Mind or human.

Actions:
Consequential changes requiring approval or showing verified executed actions.

When a task produces an action:

- The task becomes Awaiting Action Approval.
- The action is linked to the same task and execution.
- The action appears inside the task.
- It also appears in the Action Approval Centre.
- Approving from either view updates the same record.
- The execution resumes.
- The task completes only after verification.

Do not create unrelated task and action records with matching titles.
