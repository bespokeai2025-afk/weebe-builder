# 3. STOP REPORTING STATISTICS WITHOUT DECISIONS

HiveMind and GrowthMind must not treat statistics as recommendations.

The following are observations, not decisions:

* Total spend.
* Impressions.
* Clicks.
* CTR.
* CPC.
* Conversion totals.
* Reach.
* Engagement.
* Keyword count.
* Campaign count.

Every high-priority marketing message must answer:

1. What is wrong or what opportunity exists?
2. What evidence supports the conclusion?
3. What is the likely root cause?
4. Why does it matter commercially?
5. What exact action should be taken?
6. What financial or commercial result is expected?
7. What are the risks?
8. What information is uncertain?
9. Can WEBEE execute the change?
10. Does approval need to be obtained?

Example:

“£312 has been spent on search terms that produced no qualified leads. Most of this spend came from informational searches rather than businesses looking to buy an AI receptionist service. I recommend adding 18 negative keywords, reducing two broad-match bids and moving £20 per day into the campaign producing booked demonstrations. These changes are low risk and reversible. Shall I prepare the exact changes for approval?”

The system must lead with the decision, not the metric.
