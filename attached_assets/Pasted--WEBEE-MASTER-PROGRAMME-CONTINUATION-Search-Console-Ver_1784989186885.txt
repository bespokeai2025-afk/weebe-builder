   - Browser storage

# 9. AI SEO SAFETY GATE

Every article and website change must pass a mandatory SEO Safety Gate.

## Originality

Detect and block:

- Copied competitor content
- Excessive similarity to external sources
- Excessive similarity to existing website pages
- Near-identical articles
- Repeated introductions
- Repeated conclusions
- Synonymised copies
- Near-identical location pages
- Duplicate topic targeting
- Keyword cannibalisation

## Usefulness

Require:

- A real user problem
- A complete answer
- Original insight
- Business-specific detail
- Product relevance
- Useful examples
- No filler solely for article length
- No sections written only to repeat queries
- A genuine reason for the content to exist

## Factuality

Block:

- Invented statistics
- Invented quotes
- Invented testimonials
- Invented customer results
- Unsupported product capabilities
- Unsupported savings claims
- Incorrect pricing
- Outdated claims
- Legal guarantees
- Ranking guarantees
- False competitor claims

## Privacy and consent

Block:

- Private lead information
- Personal customer data
- Unapproved call transcript content
- Unapproved customer names
- Unapproved case studies
- Unapproved recordings
- Sensitive CRM data

## Technical SEO

Validate:

- URL
- Title
- Meta description
- H1
- Heading hierarchy
- Canonical
- Robots
- Indexability
- Internal links
- Broken links
- Image alt text
- Structured data syntax
- Sitemap requirement
- Mobile suitability
- Duplicate metadata

## Website protection

Ensure:

- No current routes are accidentally removed
- No accidental noindex
- No accidental robots disallow
- No accidental canonical to another website
- No unapproved sitewide change
- No broken site templates
- No deletion without approval
- A rollback package exists

## Prohibited automatic SEO behaviour

Never allow:

- Hundreds of automatic pages
- Doorway pages
- Near-identical city pages
- Keyword stuffing
- Hidden text
- Competitor article rewriting
- Search-result scraping converted into articles
- Fake reviews
- Fake authors
- Fake dates
- Automated backlink spam
- Automated comment spam
- Automatic publication after a failed quality check
- Mass URL creation
- Sitewide metadata changes without approval
- Canonical changes without approval
- Robots changes without approval
- Redirects without approval
- Page deletion without approval
- Sitemap deletion without approval

## Location-page controls

A country or city page may only be created when it contains genuinely different value such as:

- Different service availability
- Different language
- Different phone-number capability
- Different regulations
- Different customer examples
- Different operational model
- Different customer problems
- Different offers

Do not generate location pages where only the place name changes.

## Approval defaults

Default to:

- Strategy generation allowed
- Brief drafting allowed
- Article drafting allowed
- Content approval required
- Deployment approval required
- High-risk technical approval mandatory
- No bulk automatic publishing

Mandatory human approval for:

- Robots
- Noindex
- Canonicals
- Redirects
- URL changes
- Deletions
- Sitewide templates
- Structured data involving claims
- Mass page creation
- Increased publishing limits
- Location-page campaigns
