# PHASE 2 — CAMPAIGN DIAGNOSIS

Produce a proper diagnosis with supporting figures.

The diagnosis must answer:

1. Is the campaign receiving enough traffic to judge performance?
2. Is the CTR strong or weak compared with the campaign’s own ad groups and keywords?
3. Is CPC increasing or decreasing?
4. Is the campaign limited by budget?
5. Is it limited by ad rank?
6. Are search terms commercially relevant?
7. Are broad-match keywords wasting spend?
8. Are high-intent queries being captured?
9. Are ads closely matched to keyword intent?
10. Are landing pages closely matched to the ads?
11. Is conversion tracking reliable?
12. Are mobile and desktop performance materially different?
13. Are locations producing different results?
14. Are certain days or hours wasting spend?
15. Is the campaign structure too broad?
16. Are unrelated services combined in one campaign or ad group?
17. Are keywords competing against each other?
18. Are negative keywords missing?
19. Are ads receiving meaningful impressions but weak engagement?
20. Is the campaign ready for scaling, or should it first be corrected?

Every conclusion must refer to real evidence.

Do not use statements such as:

- improve targeting
- optimise keywords
- create better ads
- improve the landing page

unless the report explains exactly what is wrong and exactly what should change.
